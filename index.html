Frequently asked questions

How do I follow a team?Open the Teams page from the menu, tap on any team card, and select "Follow" to get live updates and notifications for that team.
Why is the live score delayed?Scores are updated in near real-time. A short delay of 10-15 seconds can occur depending on your network connection.
How does Fantasy Cricket scoring work?Points are awarded for runs, wickets, catches and strike rate. Your Captain earns 2x points and Vice-Captain earns 1.5x points.
How do I change notification settings?Go to Menu → Settings → Notifications and toggle the alerts you want to receive.
Still need help?Reach out to us at support@criclive.com and our team will respond within 24 hours.
